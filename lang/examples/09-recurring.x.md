# Recurring tasks

@every repeats a task: day, week, month, year, or a whole-day duration like 2w.

- [ ] Review finances @every(month) @due(2026-09-30)
- [ ] Water the plants @every(3d) @due(2026-09-18)
- [ ] Back up photos @every(2w) @due(2026-09-21)
